# Patent Commitment

*Version 1.0 — applies to the ELF Format Specification and all subsequent
versions published in this repository.*

## 1. Commitment

Each Contributor to this specification irrevocably commits, on behalf of
itself and its successors, not to assert any of its Essential Claims against
any party for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing, or
distributing any Conforming Implementation of the specification.

This commitment is royalty-free, worldwide, non-exclusive, and applies to
all versions of the specification published in this repository, including
drafts.

## 2. Definitions

- **Contributor** means the editors of this specification and any person or
  entity whose contribution is incorporated into the specification.
- **Essential Claims** means claims of a patent or patent application that a
  Contributor owns or controls, which are necessarily infringed by
  implementing the normative portions of the specification, where there is
  no commercially reasonable non-infringing alternative. Essential Claims do
  not cover implementation techniques that the specification does not
  require, or the implementation of other specifications referenced herein.
- **Conforming Implementation** means software or hardware that implements
  the normative requirements of the specification, in whole or in the
  conformance classes defined by the specification.

## 3. Defensive suspension

This commitment is suspended with respect to any party that files, maintains,
or voluntarily participates in a patent infringement lawsuit asserting an
Essential Claim (or any patent claim reading on a Conforming Implementation)
against a Contributor or against any Conforming Implementation. Suspension is
limited to the party bringing the claim and takes effect as of the filing
date of the claim.

## 4. Relationship to Apache-2.0

Code, schemas, and test material in this repository are separately licensed
under the Apache License 2.0, which contains its own patent grant (Section 3)
and defensive termination clause. This document extends a comparable
royalty-free commitment to the specification itself, so that independent
implementations that copy nothing from this repository are equally covered.

## 5. No other rights

Except as expressly stated here and in the licenses accompanying this
repository, no other rights are granted, whether by implication, estoppel,
or otherwise. This is a commitment not to assert; it is not an assignment
or license of any patent.

## 6. Contributions

By contributing to the specification (see CONTRIBUTING.md), each Contributor
agrees that this commitment applies to that contribution and to any Essential
Claims the Contributor holds that read on it.
